Your sign stays. Your mode changes. The weather belongs to everyone.

This is an authored, finite almanac with deterministic daily rotation. Motifs recur. It is not astronomy, measured machine conditions, empirically validated advice, or a personality assessment. Keep what is useful; discard the rest.
